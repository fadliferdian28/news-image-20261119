A one-page newspaper front page for Thursday 19 November 2026: 51 verified
stories from seven regions — the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin three-way summit
in Shenzhen, the World Court's genocide ruling against Myanmar, the COP31
draft that cut the $120bn aid goal, and the GTA VI launch — plus a UTC
timeline of the day, a numbers strip, a numbered-pin world map, and a DATE
CHECK box naming the big stories that broke on other days and were left out
on purpose.

Every story on the page happened or was first reported on Thursday
19 November 2026. Each lead and medium card carries a CLAIM or CONFIRMED
proof tag with its source. Scene panels are AI-generated illustrations;
no real people are depicted. The composition is a stack of newspapers:
the main sheet carries the full front page, with three more papers
peeking out from behind it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Copies `entry.png` (1024×1024) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or the repo
directory when unset) and verifies the copy with a sha256 comparison.
Standard library only — no dependencies.
